DoseMate privacy policy

Developer: Preethi. Privacy contact: dosemate26@gmail.com. Published: 23 November 2026. Version 1.

In short

DoseMate keeps what you enter on your own phone. It has no account, no sign-in and no server. The developer does not receive your medicines, schedules, doses, notes or pictures.

What the app says inside itself

The app shows this text in Settings, in the card called "Privacy and safety". It is the same text as on this page:

DoseMate keeps your medicines, schedules, doses, notes and pictures on this phone only, encrypted. It has no account and no server, and the developer does not receive your data. If you uninstall DoseMate or clear its data, your data and its key are deleted and cannot be recovered.

Reminders can arrive late. In testing on one phone, some reminders arrived 8 to 23 minutes after their set time. DoseMate does not use Android's exact-alarm permission, so Android and your phone's battery settings decide when a reminder is shown, and they can delay or stop it. Do not rely on DoseMate as your only reminder for essential medicines.

DoseMate is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. It does not give medical advice. Always consult a healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

What data the app stores

Only what you enter, and only if you enter it:

  • medicines: name, strength, form, colour, your own instructions, status and start and end dates;
  • schedules: times, amounts, selected days and labels;
  • dose records: taken, skipped or missed, and when;
  • stock: tablet counts and your refill alert choices;
  • optional prescription entries: a title, a doctor's name, a date and notes;
  • optional pictures: a tablet photo or a picture of a prescription;
  • the app's own settings: theme, text size and similar choices.

All of this is stored on your phone only. The medicine data is kept in an encrypted database and the pictures are stored encrypted. The key is kept in your phone's protected storage.

What the developer receives

Nothing. DoseMate does not collect, send, sell or share your data with the developer or with any third party. It has no account, no sign-in and no server. It contains no advertising code and no analytics code. Android or Google Play may give the developer anonymised install, crash or usage statistics outside the app, under Google's own rules.

Sharing

You can choose "Share this report". DoseMate first shows you the exact text that would be sent. Medicine names are left out unless you turn them on each time. The text is handed to Android's share sheet and goes nowhere unless you choose an app there. What that app does with the text is outside DoseMate and is covered by that app's own policy.

Permissions

DoseMate asks Android for these permissions, which Android names as follows: post notifications, receive boot completed, and prevent the phone from sleeping (wake lock). They are used for the app's reminders. DoseMate does not ask for camera, storage, contacts, location or microphone permission. Pictures are chosen with Android's own camera app or photo picker. If you pick or take a picture, the original stays where Android saved it; DoseMate does not delete it.

Reminders

Reminder notifications show only "DoseMate" and "Time for a reminder". They never show a medicine name. What a locked phone displays also depends on your phone's own notification settings.

Reminders can arrive late. In testing on one phone, some reminders arrived 8 to 23 minutes after their set time. DoseMate does not use Android's exact-alarm permission, so Android and your phone's battery settings decide when a reminder is shown, and they can delay or stop it. Do not rely on DoseMate as your only reminder for essential medicines.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. You can delete a medicine, a prescription entry or a picture inside the app. If you uninstall DoseMate or clear its data, your data and its key are deleted and cannot be recovered. The developer cannot recover them either. Because the developer holds none of your data, there is nothing for the developer to delete on your request.

Not medical advice

DoseMate is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. It does not give medical advice. Always consult a healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Children

DoseMate is not designed for children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version and its date will replace this page. The version number and the publication date at the top show which text you are reading.
